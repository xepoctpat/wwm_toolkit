## Editorial normalization rules used

- Region summaries are rewritten in original wording rather than copied from official or community prose.
- Only the three currently surfaced regions (`Qinghe`, `Kaifeng`, `Hexi`) are modeled in this seed slice.
- Subregions and landmark lists stay intentionally conservative: they are used to support search, routing, and future map overlays, not to imply 100% region completion.
- Traversal summaries describe route character and content density rather than pretending to be exact collectible walkthroughs.
- Published region gates can be modeled explicitly even when they are not a tidy ascending level ladder; region order and character-level requirements are related metadata, not interchangeable concepts.
- Literal `Encounter` pins stay distinct from `Exploration Guide`, `Hidden Paths`, `Side Story`, `Martial Art`, and `Mystic Skill` layers in the reviewed notes and should not be collapsed into one generic optional-content label.
- When reviewed quest entries exist, region detail views should surface exploration routes, Jianghu Legacy / mystic-skill detours, campaign quests, and encounters as separate layers rather than merging them into one optional-content bucket.
- Region entries use the same slugs already present in `quest.regionIds` and `storyline.regionIds` so existing references become first-class document links.
